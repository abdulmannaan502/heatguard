HeatGuard: personal heat-strain early warning for outdoor workers
Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge, [Population Health] Climate-Resilient Worker Health

HeatGuard estimates each worker's core temperature from a low-cost wearable heart rate, site weather, task and days on the job, forecasts it 30 minutes ahead, and sends a tiered alert (amber or red) in the worker's own language. Supervisors see only the tier. Authorities see aggregates of 5 or more workers.

Everything shown in this video is simulated (synthetic workers, physics-based simulator). HeatGuard is screening and decision support, not a diagnosis or a medical device. Alert translations are machine-drafted and need native-speaker review before any pilot.
